A curated collection of public, academic, government, economic, educational, geographic, international, and humanitarian data sources for research and data visualization projects.

General and Public Data Sources

Data.gov

The U.S. government's central open-data portal, providing access to datasets from federal, state, and local agencies.

Visit Data.gov

Data Liberation Project

A public-interest project focused on obtaining and publishing government datasets that may otherwise be difficult to access.

Visit Data Liberation Project

Beautiful Public Data

A collection of noteworthy public datasets and examples of useful and visually compelling public information.

Visit Beautiful Public Data

Information Is Beautiful — Data

Data resources associated with Information Is Beautiful, useful for visualization projects and data storytelling.

Visit Information Is Beautiful

Florida Geospatial Open Data Portal

Geographic and spatial datasets for the State of Florida, including boundaries, infrastructure, environmental data, maps, and other GIS resources.

Visit Florida Geospatial Open Data

Miami-Dade County GIS Open Data

Miami-Dade County GIS datasets covering geography, planning, infrastructure, demographics, and local government data.

Visit Miami-Dade GIS Open Data

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Official U.S. data on employment, unemployment, wages, inflation, productivity, occupations, and labor-market conditions.

Visit BLS Data

World Bank Open Data

Global development indicators covering economics, population, poverty, health, education, infrastructure, trade, and related topics.

Visit World Bank Open Data

FIU Libraries — Databases

Florida International University library databases for scholarly research, statistics, publications, and specialized data sources.

Visit FIU Libraries Databases

U.S. Department of Education data tools covering schools, colleges, enrollment, student outcomes, demographics, and education statistics.

Our World in Data

Research and datasets covering global health, population, economics, energy, environment, education, technology, and social issues.

Visit Our World in Data

United Nations data related to the Sustainable Development Goals, including social, economic, environmental, and development indicators.
